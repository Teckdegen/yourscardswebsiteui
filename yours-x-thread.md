# YOURS thread for X

Post each block as its own post, in order. First line is the hook. No hyphens. Light on the mechanics.

---

What if the money you move today could help cover an expense tomorrow?

You buy something. You pay a bill. You swipe a card. You send from a wallet. The payment clears. The story ends.

What if it did not?

---

That is the idea behind YOURS.

Your financial activity should not only move money. It should create value that can come back to you.

---

Today, $100 spent is $100 gone.

You might get a few points. You might get a little cashback. Then the transaction is finished.

YOURS looks at that same swipe as history.

---

Your spending builds your Yours Score.

The Score is not money. It is not a points balance. You do not redeem it for cash.

It is your reputation with YOURS. It shows how you use the platform over time, and it helps decide what you can access.

---

Then you make a purchase tomorrow.

You use your YOURS card the normal way. The merchant gets paid. Nothing changes at checkout.

After it clears, YOURS checks your Score, your activity, and the coverage reserve.

If the transaction qualifies, YOURS covers it. The money is sent back to your wallet.

Covered by Yours ✓

---

You did not apply for a loan.

You did not enter a repayment plan.

You made a transaction. The activity you already built gave you access to something back.

---

This is not credit.

Credit lends you money today and asks you to pay it back later.

YOURS is a different relationship. What you do today can make an expense coverable tomorrow.

---

The money, simply.

YOURS takes a 3% tax. That tax funds the bill coverage reserve.

Your Score decides if a transaction can be covered, and how much.
The reserve holds the capital.
The transaction is the event.

---

Picture someone who uses the card the same way every week.

Subscriptions. Groceries. Software. Online shops. Everyday buys.

That history is the reputation. One day they swipe again, and YOURS can look at the whole picture, not only the last payment, and cover it.

---

Cashback gives you a small slice of one purchase.
Credit gives you money you have to repay.

YOURS connects the way you already spend with a chance to have a later bill covered.

---

Your reputation should not only say who you are.

It should give you something back.

Your transactions today can help cover your expenses tomorrow.

Your money stays Yours.
